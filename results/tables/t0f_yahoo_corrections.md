| Date | Company | Return in Yahoo's adjusted prices | Return used | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| whole sample | SITE Centers Corp. | – | – | Adjusted prices not used (close without dividends instead): SITE Centers (formerly DDR): each quarterly dividend takes about 10% off the adjusted price |
| 2011-11-01 | ITT Inc. | +24% | +0% | Spin-off of Xylem and Exelis and share consolidation: +24% in Yahoo |

| 2017-01-03 | Xerox Holdings Corporation | +38% | +0% | Spin-off of Conduent: +38% in Yahoo |

Yahoo's adjusted prices are right on the day of an ordinary dividend or split, but a few spin-offs and mergers are adjusted twice, with the wrong ratio or not at all. They were found by comparing the adjusted price with the unadjusted one and by looking at the largest one-day moves; others, smaller, may remain.
