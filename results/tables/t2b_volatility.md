### Where the volatility comes from: the stocks held and how they are combined

| Portfolio | Average volatility of the stocks held | Diversification ratio | Volatility of the portfolio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Most Diversified Portfolio | 31.3% | 2.36 | 13.9% |
| Minimum variance | 22.3% | 2.01 | 11.6% |
| Equal weight | 29.2% | 1.86 | 16.5% |

Averages of the out-of-sample windows (the volatility of a window is lower than that of the whole period, which also has the changes from one window to the next). The volatility of a portfolio is the average volatility of its stocks divided by its diversification ratio. The MDP divides by the largest number, but it starts from stocks that are more volatile than the average; minimum variance starts from the calm ones.
